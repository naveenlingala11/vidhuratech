Rules & Best Practices

Placement Preparation Guidelines

Cracking placements requires matching both automated scoring algorithms and human recruiter standards.

ATS Content Guidelines

Use Standard Section Headings:

Stick to recognized titles like "Professional Experience", "Skills", "Education", and "Projects". Avoid creative titles like "Where I've Been" or "My Superpowers".

Quantify Your Accomplishments:

Always use metrics. Replace "Developed APIs for database management" with "Developed REST APIs using Spring Boot, reducing database query latencies by 45%".

Don't Put Key Contact Details in Headers/Footers:

Many ATS parsers ignore header/footer fields. Place your email, phone, and GitHub links at the very top of the main document body.

Don't Use Progress Ratings as Images:

Star ratings, percentage bars, and visual dots inside skills sections are invisible to parsers. Use text density instead (e.g. "Languages: Java, TypeScript, Python").

Placement Verification Checklist

Single Page Limit:

Keep your resume strictly to 1 page if you have less than 5 years of experience. Use our Customizer's spacing slider to adjust padding to fit.

Spelling & Grammar Double-Check:

Ensure there are zero typos. Automated tools reject resumes with multiple spelling errors immediately.

Hyperlinks Verification:

Test all GitHub, LinkedIn, and portfolio links. Ensure they are fully public, working, and lead to active projects.
