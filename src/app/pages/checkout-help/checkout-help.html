Step-by-Step

How Checkout and Enrollment Works

Follow these 4 simple steps to secure your batch seat and activate your interactive lms coding playgrounds.

01

Select Batch & Plan

Choose your target live training program and preferred batch date (Morning/Evening) on the course page, then click "Enroll Now".

02

Billing Details

Provide your name, active mobile number, and email. Double-check your details as invoices and course access credentials are linked to this email.

03

Razorpay Secure Payment

Choose your preferred payment method (UPI, Cards, EMI, Netbanking) on our secure 256-bit encrypted checkout popup managed by Razorpay.

04

Instant Activation

Upon success, your account upgrades instantly. Check your mailbox for the welcome kit, credentials, and tax invoice. Go to your Dashboard to begin.

Invoices & Tax Receipts

All payments made on Vidhura Tech generate an instant official tax invoice. Here is what you need to know about invoices:

  • Instant Email Delivery: A PDF copy of your tax invoice is mailed within 60 seconds of a successful checkout.
  • Dashboard Archives: You can view, search, and download all past transaction invoices from your student settings panel under "Billing History".
  • Corporate Reimbursements: If your company sponsors your training, you can add your company GSTIN during checkout to request custom tax invoices.

Batch & Course Enrollments

Once you are enrolled, our LMS configures your learning tracks. Here are the enrollment guidelines:

  • Batch Freezing: Your batch timings are secured at checkout. Batch change requests can be raised via dashboard within 7 days of course start.
  • Lifetime Access: You get lifetime access to recorded classes, coding sandboxes, quizzes, and future batch updates.
  • Mentor Allocation: An industry mentor is auto-allocated to your profile within 2 hours of payment success for 1-on-1 guidance.
