Avoid Common ATS Mistakes

BAD: Complex Graphics

Using graphical rating bars, progress circles, or complex 2-column tables to list skills.

Java ●●●●○
Why: ATS parsers read progress dots as empty spaces or random symbols, ignoring your skill entirely.
GOOD: Text Tags & Comma Lists

Listing skills as plain text tags or a comma-separated list under clear headings.

Java Spring Boot SQL
Why: 100% readable by all ATS parsers, allowing indexers to match keywords immediately.

Understand Your ATS Match Index

Our scanner matches keywords and checks structural alignment. Here is how to interpret your score:

Below 60% Critical Action Needed

High chance of automatic rejection. Add missing skills and rewrite experience bullets with action verbs.

60% - 80% Needs Alignment

Good base. Use the Auto-Optimizer or AI suggestions to add 4-5 key missing technologies and roles.

Above 80% ATS Ready ✓

Excellent match! Your resume has high search visibility for recruiters sourcing for this role.
