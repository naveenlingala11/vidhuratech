🔗 Complete Platform Integration & Data Flow

How Live Connect integrates with the Candidate, Mentor, and Trainer dashboards across the VidhuraTech project.

Step 1
🏠

Quick Creator / Booking

Public users generate room URLs from the Home Page quick creator. Alternatively, logged-in candidates request scheduled mock sessions via Mentor availability slots.

Step 2
🔑

Lobby Identification

Hosts and guest candidates hit the `/meeting/:roomName` route. Logged-in accounts bypass directly, while new guest users must submit details at the authorization gate.

Step 3
📞

WebRTC Session Init

Loads Element Call's secure script. Toggles sync local mic, cam, and presenter states with Jitsi iframe APIs. Video indicators reflect active streaming states.

Step 4
📊

Live Review & Score

Side-by-side dashboard opens. Interviewers view candidate resumes, run scratchpad coding tests, compile logic, and grade criteria on score sliders.

Step 5
💾

End Call & Auto-Save

Clicking "End Call" terminates calling channels, initiates a local download of the WebM screen recording file, and brings up the feedback rating dashboard.

Terms of Service & Room Rules

Guidelines to maintain a highly professional, secure, and ethical training environment for all session attendees.

🛡️

1. Session Conduct Code

All hosts, students, and mentor participants are expected to maintain professional behavior. Any form of harassment, inappropriate language, or sharing of copyrighted assessment materials is strictly prohibited and will result in access bans.

💾

2. Client-Side Recording Policy

Our local WebM screen recorder captures sessions directly within the host's browser using HTML5 display media capture. Since these video recordings are stored purely in your browser's temporary memory buffers and downloaded locally to your computer, **VidhuraTech does not store or access your session files** on our databases, ensuring total data privacy.

🔒

3. Guest Entry Gate Verification

While registration is not required to join a mock interview, new unauthenticated guest users must fill out their full name, email address, and designated role on our glassmorphic gate screen before gaining access to the room. This secures call channels from unauthorized bot joins.

📄

4. Content Ownership & IP

All algorithmic questions, system design interview templates, and custom evaluation rubric patterns provided in the VidhuraTech question bank remain the exclusive intellectual property of VidhuraTech. Redistribution is strictly forbidden.

🔒 Privacy Assurance

Because Free Tier sessions utilize secure, end-to-end peer WebRTC protocols via Element Call integrations at meet.element.io, your video feeds, audio channels, and code scratchpad content are directly streamed between participants. Your private conversations and proprietary code solutions are never cached or uploaded to our servers.
