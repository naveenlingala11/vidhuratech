1. Overview & Consent

This Cookie Policy explains how Vidhura Tech uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage parameters, web beacons, and pixels) to recognize you when you visit our coding platform, practice sandboxes, and dashboard environments.

It describes what these technologies are, why we use them, and the rights you have to control our usage of them on your browser systems.

Consent Indicator:

By continuing to navigate the Vidhura Tech platform, practice exercises, or access sandbox code workspaces, you explicitly consent to our storage of cookies as detailed in this policy.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files containing unique identifiers that are sent to your web browser and saved in your computer or mobile device memory when you access our portal. They enable websites to recall key telemetry inputs and preferences:

  • Storage Mechanics: Saved locally within the user's specific browser directory. They are passed back to our servers on subsequent requests.
  • Safe Execution: Cookies are flat text records; they cannot install viruses, execute malicious scripts, or access local files on your machine.
  • Duration Types: Can be "session-based" (temporary records cleared when the browser is closed) or "persistent" (remaining until they expire or are manually cleared).

3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to streamline platform usability, secure student authentication sessions, and gather traffic stats to reduce platform loading delays:

  • Persistent Authentication: Keeping you logged in as you navigate between curriculum outlines, job boards, and sandbox panels.
  • Practice Editor Settings: Retaining your customized coding layout preferences, sound settings, and tab size values.
  • Security Audits: Recognizing repetitive login failure events to safeguard active accounts from brute-force attempts.
  • Performance Optimization: Tracking asset caching parameters to ensure faster file loading rates.

4. Types of Cookies We Use

The cookies stored by our platform fall into four categories depending on their function:

  • Essential & Critical Cookies: Strictly required to provision core services like secure payment redirects, session token storage, and sandbox loading states. The platform cannot function without these.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Used to track page views, interface lag levels, and site navigation paths anonymously. This data helps our engineers optimize dashboard layouts.
  • Preferences & Customization: Saving settings such as code compilation options, selected coding fonts, and volume control levels.
  • Functional Integrations: Powering helper widgets like live support chat logs and placement application wizards.

5. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our first-party cookies, trusted third-party partners may place tracking scripts on your device to enable external functionality:

  • Google Analytics: Collects web traffic flow details anonymously to help us understand which training topics are in highest demand.
  • Razorpay Gateway: Uses session cookies to verify transactions and prevent payment duplication errors.
  • Social & Chat Widgets: Placed by help APIs to coordinate real-time chats with mentor resources.

6. How You Can Control Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or block cookies on your devices. You can customize your preferences through the following steps:

  • Browser Preferences: Adjusting browser privacy options (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) to reject all cookies or request warnings before saving.
  • Manual Data Clearing: Regularly clearing stored site cookies from your browser history settings to delete persistent identifiers.
  • Browser Extensions: Using privacy blockers or tracking shield add-ons to prevent third-party scripts from executing.
Important Consideration:

If you disable essential first-party cookies, major parts of the Vidhura Tech platform—including interactive sandboxes, assessment timers, and account checkouts—will not operate correctly.
