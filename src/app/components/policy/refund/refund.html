1. Overview & Scope

At Vidhura Tech, our primary commitment is to provide state-of-the-art interactive coding environments, enterprise-grade syllabus, and 1-on-1 mentorship. This Cancellation and Refund Policy defines the circumstances under which student fees may be refunded or batches changed. It protects both the student's learning investment and the operational schedules of our expert trainers.

Important Note:

By enrolling in any batch or program on the Vidhura Tech platform, you explicitly agree to the terms defined in this Cancellation and Refund Policy.

2. General Policy Terms

Vidhura Tech provides live batches, sandbox playgrounds, assessments, and mentorship. Once you secure a seat in a batch, resources are allocated immediately.

  • Non-Refundable Standard: Training fees paid to Vidhura Tech are non-refundable unless specified otherwise in this policy document.
  • Service Activation: Access to course materials, interactive sandbox labs, or attending a single live batch session confirms service initiation.
  • Active Sessions: Refunds cannot be requested for ongoing batches or completed courses.

3. Eligibility for Refund

Refunds are considered only under exceptional circumstances and are subject to verification by the Vidhura Tech management team:

  • Duplicate Transaction: If you were charged twice for the same enrollment due to payment gateway lag, the duplicate amount will be refunded completely.
  • Technical System Blocks: If a technical issue on the Vidhura Tech platform prevents you from accessing the LMS, and our support team fails to resolve it within 72 hours, you are eligible for a full refund.
  • Course Cancellation: In the rare event that Vidhura Tech cancels a batch, enrolled students will receive a 100% refund or can choose to transfer to another batch.
  • Personal Emergency: Extreme medical cases or personal emergencies (subject to certified documentary proof) will be reviewed by management on a case-by-case basis.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Vidhura Tech will not approve refund requests in the following scenarios:

  • Change of Mind: If you change your mind after securing a seat or choose to discontinue study without a technical issue.
  • Inability to Attend: Inability to attend classes due to personal time constraints, changing office hours, or college exam schedules.
  • Dissatisfaction: Dissatisfaction without reasonable, documented justification or failure to complete pre-requisites.
  • Partial Progress: If you have completed more than 1 week of training, attended more than 2 live classes, or solved sandbox assignments.

5. Refund Request Process

To submit a refund request, follow these steps to ensure quick processing:

1
Send an Email Request

Email support@vidhuratech.com with the subject line Refund Request: [Course Name] - [Your Name].

2
Include Transaction Info

Provide your Payment ID, Razorpay Transaction ID, Invoice Number, and registered Email Address.

3
Attach Supporting Proof

Attach screenshots of technical errors or official certified proof for personal emergencies.

6. Processing Duration

Approved refunds are processed through secure merchant bank gateways:

  • Timeframe: Refund amounts take 5 to 10 business days to reflect in your original payment source.
  • Method: Refunds are sent exclusively to the original bank account, card, or UPI ID used during checkout.
  • Charges: Any merchant transaction fees or local taxes charged during checkout are non-refundable and will be deducted.

7. Course Cancellation by Us

If a batch start is delayed by more than 15 days or cancelled by Vidhura Tech due to trainer availability or technical reasons:

  • You will get an option to join the next batch immediately.
  • Or request a full 100% refund without any deductions.
