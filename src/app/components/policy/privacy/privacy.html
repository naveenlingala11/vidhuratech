1. Overview & Consent

At Vidhura Tech, we are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring transparency in how your data is collected, processed, and safeguarded. We value the trust you place in us and aim to follow global standards in user data privacy.

This Privacy Policy governs the collection and usage of data for all users who access, register, or purchase training services, sandbox instances, and assessments on the Vidhura Tech platform.

Consent & Acknowledgement:

By creating an account, registering for our batches, or accessing our sandbox playground services, you explicitly consent to the data collection and processing methods described in this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver a personalized interactive learning experience, database sandbox labs, and course track progress, we gather both personal and non-personal data:

  • Identity & Profile Information: Your full name, registered email address, primary contact number, educational qualifications, and course track preferences.
  • Authentication Credentials: Securely encrypted password hashes, OAuth tokens (if logging in via Google or third-party OAuth providers), and login access timestamps.
  • Billing & Payments: Details related to course enrollment purchases, invoice billing history, transaction IDs (e.g. Razorpay logs). Note: We do not store raw card numbers or CVVs on our servers; payments are processed securely by PCI-DSS compliant providers.
  • Interactive Sandbox & Workspace Logs: Source code written in our code playgrounds, sandbox terminal actions, assessment attempts, and code completion logs.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, session duration, click patterns, and page navigation logs to optimize platform latency and interface styling.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to operate, maintain, and continuously improve the Vidhura Tech educational ecosystem:

  • Service Delivery: Assigning seats in live trainer batches, provisioning database sandboxes, displaying practice leaderboards, and grading assessment submissions.
  • Account Operations: Processing course checkout transactions, generating PDF billing invoices, and sending enrollment completion notices.
  • Communications: Dispatching notifications regarding class timing alterations, mock interview reminders, new placement postings, and password updates.
  • Platform Personalization: Suggesting relevant coding practice labs, maintaining code editor preferences, and tailoring tutor feedback.
  • Analytics & Safety: Detecting malicious code submissions, avoiding double-login attempts, tracking platform bug errors, and analyzing site optimization stats.

4. Data Protection & Security

The security of your personal information is extremely important to us. We implement multiple layers of security to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure:

  • Encrypted Communications: All network requests and data transfers are protected under secure SSL/HTTPS protocols.
  • Secure Server Architecture: Databases are hosted in secure networks behind strict firewall configurations with restricted console access.
  • Identity Security: We enforce strong password guidelines and secure session authorization mechanisms.
Your Responsibility:

While we use industry-standard mechanisms to secure your data, no network transmission can be 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and avoiding account sharing.

5. Sharing of Information

We respect your privacy and will never sell, lease, or rent your personal information to third-party marketing companies for promotional purposes. We only share data in the following scoped situations:

  • With Service Providers: Sharing billing info with payment gateways (Razorpay), communication details with email dispatchers (SendGrid/SMTP), and hosting data with cloud servers.
  • For Placements & Referrals: With your prior approval, sharing mock assessment scores and resumes with partner recruiters looking for qualified developers.
  • Legal Enforcement: If required by court orders, local government regulations, or when necessary to protect the security of our platform and users.

6. Third-Party Services & Integrations

Our web applications reference or utilize external services. Each third-party platform governs their data through independent regulations:

  • Google Analytics: Implemented to track demographic summaries and site interaction rates anonymously.
  • Google OAuth: To provide secure, single-click signup processes.
  • WhatsApp API Integration: To assist you with direct support chats and query resolutions.

We encourage you to review the privacy documentation of these third-party platforms before engaging with their features.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies (small text files saved on your computer) and browser local storage to maintain session states and user preferences:

  • Session Management: Storing authentication state so you do not need to sign in repeatedly on every page load.
  • User Customizations: Saving your dark/light theme choices, code playground editor layout selections, and sound preferences.
  • Performance Optimization: Caching resource paths to reduce server request loads.

You can configure your browser settings to reject cookies or warn you before they are saved. However, disabling cookies will prevent major sections of the LMS and sandbox features from operating correctly.

8. Your Rights & Control

As a student or user on our platform, you hold control over your personal data:

  • Access & Correction: You can view and edit your profile parameters, name, and contact details from the dashboard settings page.
  • Communications Preferences: You can unsubscribe from system newsletters or newsletter announcements by clicking unsubscribe links in emails.
  • Account Deactivation: You can contact support to request temporary block or permanent deletion of your user account records (subject to active fee clearance conditions).

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may modify this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological adjustments, new feature integrations, or legal revisions. The updated policy will be posted on this page with the modified date. Continued platform access after revisions confirms your agreement to the modified rules.
