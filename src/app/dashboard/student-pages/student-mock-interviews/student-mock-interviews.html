Interview Studio Guide

Mock Interview Booking & Preparation Guidelines

Follow these structural steps to schedule, practice, and review your progress successfully:

01

Specify Topic & Role

Select your active cohort batch and name the target area (e.g. "React Hooks" or "HR round").

02

Select Preferred Slot

Set a date and time, and add remarks listing specific concepts you'd like to get tested on.

03

Check Schedule Link

Monitor your timeline. Once assigned, you will see a meeting button to connect live.

04

Attend & Audit reviews

Conduct your mock call. Post-session, read detailed trainer feedback points and ratings right here.
