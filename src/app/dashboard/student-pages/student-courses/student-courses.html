Guide & Workflow

Student Progression Guidelines

Follow this step-by-step path to successfully complete your programs, prepare for placements, and claim verified credentials.

01

Select Course Card

Browse through your active learning cohorts in the dashboard and click "Enter Learning Space" to navigate to your class player.

02

Check Curriculum

Navigate to the Curriculum tab inside the player to view upcoming topics, reference guides, and syllabus milestones uploaded by the trainer.

03

Watch & Code Along

Streaming bookmarks are updated every 5 seconds. Watch sessions fully and complete coding assignments posted by your mentors.

04

Earn Certificate

After finishing 100% of your curriculum criteria, mark the modules as complete and generate your PDF Certificate from the credentials page.
